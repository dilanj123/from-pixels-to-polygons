# G9-D042-DISPOSITION — GFX-026

## Disposition

**D-042 — REJECTED FOR RETENTION ON CURRENT EVIDENCE.** The active triangle
setup is the D-041 three-stage implementation. The D-042 four-stage design
remains a valid, functionally verified historical trial; rejection is a
current baseline-selection decision, not a claim that product/subtraction
pipelining is inherently ineffective.

The comparable completed routes report 52.8290 MHz for D-041 (18.929 ns,
approximately −2.263 ns slack) and 51.1587 MHz for D-042 (19.547 ns,
−2.881 ns slack) against the same 60 MHz system target. D-042 used 7,888 FF
versus D-041's 7,277, an increase of 611 FF. D-042's worst-path-only report
identifies a path through the pinned Sobel parent; it does not quantify the
new AREA path's delay or ranking. Thus the AREA-path benefit remains
unquantified. Both routes miss 60 MHz and Gate 9 remains OPEN.
